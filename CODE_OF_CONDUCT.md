# Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

## Our commitment / 我们的承诺

SlimLuma welcomes contributors and users of every background, identity,
experience level, language, and ability. We are committed to a respectful,
harassment-free community focused on building safe, local-first software.

SlimLuma 欢迎不同背景、身份、经验、语言和能力的贡献者与用户。我们致力于建设一个
相互尊重、没有骚扰、专注于安全本地软件的社区。

## Expected behavior / 期望行为

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Respect privacy. Use synthetic or redacted files and logs.
- Make room for language differences and ask for clarification in good faith.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes openly.

- 保持尊重、耐心，并清楚描述问题。
- 讨论观点与证据，不攻击个人。
- 尊重隐私，使用合成或脱敏的文件与日志。
- 包容语言差异，对不明确的表达先善意确认。
- 接受建设性反馈，并公开修正错误。

## Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受行为

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing
private media or credentials, deliberate disruption, and retaliation are not
accepted in project spaces.

项目空间不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视、性化关注、人肉搜索、公开私密媒体或凭据、故意破坏
讨论秩序及报复行为。

## Scope / 适用范围

This policy applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions,
release comments, and other spaces where someone represents SlimLuma.

本准则适用于仓库、Issue、Pull Request、讨论、Release 评论，以及以 SlimLuma 名义
参与的其他空间。

## Reporting and enforcement / 报告与处理

Do not place sensitive evidence in a public issue. Open a minimal issue asking
for a private maintainer contact, or use GitHub's private reporting channel
when applicable. Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be
reviewed as privately and impartially as the available channel permits.

请勿在公开 Issue 中放入敏感证据。可以创建一个不含细节的最小 Issue，请维护者提供
私下联系方式；适用时也可使用 GitHub 私密报告渠道。违反本准则时，维护者可删除内容，
并临时或永久限制参与。报告会在现有渠道允许的范围内尽量保密、公正地处理。
